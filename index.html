Privacy Policy

RC Puzzler's Word Search App

Developer: RCInteractiveStudios
Effective date: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

RCInteractiveStudios ("we," "us," or "our") provides RC Puzzler's Word Search App (the "App"), a free Android puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the App, including information processed by advertising services.

2. Information handled by the App

The App does not require you to register an account or submit your name, email address, or other contact details to play. It saves your completed puzzles and current puzzle progress on your device so you can continue playing. We do not operate a separate server that receives this puzzle progress.

The App includes Google AdMob. Depending on your device, settings, location, and consent choices, Google's advertising technology may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • device information and identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available;
  • ad views, taps, and other advertising interactions; and
  • app-related usage, performance, and diagnostic data used for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

The App does not request your device's precise location permission.

3. Advertising and sharing

The App displays banner ads, full-screen interstitial ads between puzzles, and optional rewarded ads that can provide hints. Information described above may be processed by Google and its advertising partners to deliver ads, measure their performance, and protect against abuse. Whether ads are personalized depends on applicable law, available options, and your choices.

For more information about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

4. Consent and choices

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present consent notices and manage advertising privacy choices. Where available or required, you may revisit these choices using the App's privacy options. You can also manage certain ad settings through your Google account or Android device settings.

5. Data security

Puzzle progress is kept in the App's local storage on your device. Communications with Google advertising services use the security features provided by Google's SDKs, including encrypted transport. We cannot guarantee that any electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored puzzle progress remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it, subject to any applicable device backup or restore features. We do not maintain an online player account or server-side puzzle history. Data processed by Google or its advertising partners is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices, which are explained in their respective policies.

7. Third-party services

The App integrates Google AdMob (for advertisements) and Google's User Messaging Platform (for consent messages). These services are provided by Google and may process information under their own policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when we publish changes.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact RCInteractiveStudios at RCInteractiveStudios@gmail.com.
